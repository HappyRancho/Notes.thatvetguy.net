What is Rx Notes?

Rx Notes, also called Notes.ThatVetGuy, is an independent study-resource library maintained by the ThatVetGuy team for veterinary students. It organises credited notes, books and practical resources so students can spend less time searching.

About the project

Who is this website for?

The library is intended for veterinary students, especially BVSc & AH learners. Academic and clinical references support study and supervised learning; they do not replace a veterinarian’s advice or current clinical guidance.

What are the key features?

Browse subject notes and TANUVAS collections, an open book library, objective resources and question banks, syllabus checklists, practical folders, professional-year roadmaps and study-tool guides. Search helps you find a topic, title or author.

How should I start?

Choose your BVSc professional year, open a subject and compare its syllabus checklist with your university’s current teaching plan. Read the matching notes, then use the books and practical resources to support revision.

Choose a year and subject

How do I find a veterinary book?

Open Books and browse broad subject categories, or search by title, author or subject. Each title has its own page with available access, source and edition details. The open library is not grouped by professional year.

Explore the book library

Using the library

7 answers

Is the library free to use?

Browsing Rx Notes is free. Linked resources are controlled by their providers; availability and access settings can change. A book listed as a reading recommendation may not have a linked copy.

Where do notes and book downloads open?

Notes and many references open in Google Drive. Some book pages offer a direct PDF from an external provider. Each page states the available access. Credit and rights remain with the original authors and publishers.

Where can I find TANUVAS notes?

Use the TANUVAS directory to browse the reviewed subject and course collections, then open a title on its matching subject page. Compare original course labels with your own syllabus.

Browse TANUVAS notes

Does the year grouping match my university?

Year grouping and teaching schedules may vary. The checklists reference the VCI MSVE 2016 course contents and link to their source. Your university’s current syllabus, amendments and examination instructions take precedence.

Open syllabus checklists

How do I check a book’s edition?

Read its title and source details, then verify the title page of the linked copy. A reference cover does not guarantee the edition of every available copy. Report a mismatch if a listing needs correction.

Can I use the library without JavaScript?

Yes. Core pages, subject links, book listings, FAQs and email templates are ordinary HTML. Live search, saved checklist ticks and some convenience controls use JavaScript.

Can I pause the scrolling notice?

Yes. Open More and choose Pause under Header notice. That choice is saved on this device. The notice is also still when your device requests reduced motion, and without JavaScript.

Requests & fixes

6 answers

How do I request a missing resource?

Open Community & Support and choose Request a resource. Include the topic or title, subject, professional year and resource type. Add an author, edition or known source when useful. Review the prepared email draft and send it from your email app.

Request a resource

How do I report an error, broken link or bug?

Choose Report an error and include the issue, affected page or resource URL, date encountered and what happened. For a website bug, add your device/browser and steps to reproduce it. A name, remarks and screenshot can help us investigate.

Report an error

How do I include a screenshot?

Attach the screenshot in your email app after opening the draft, or include a screenshot link. The website does not upload or attach files. Avoid including passwords, private records or other people’s personal information.

What if the email button does not open my mail app?

Use Copy email draft, or open the template preview and copy it manually. Paste it into an email to contact@thatvetguy.net, add any attachments and send it yourself.

When will my request be reviewed?

The team reviews requests and reports as it can; there is no fixed response or publication deadline. A request does not guarantee that a resource can be added. Clear details and a working page link help us assess it.

How can I contact the team?

Email contact@thatvetguy.net. Use Contact for general questions, feedback or suggestions. Choose the matching request, error report or copyright template when your message concerns one of those topics.

Contact the team

Credits, rights & privacy

4 answers

Is this an official university or VCI website?

No. Rx Notes is an independent project maintained by the ThatVetGuy team. Listing a university resource does not imply affiliation, endorsement or ownership.

Can I contribute study material?

Yes. Send the title, subject, original source and author credit, and explain your permission to share it. Contribute only material you are entitled to share. The team may review whether it fits the library.

Contribute a resource

How do I raise a copyright or credit concern?

Email contact@thatvetguy.net with the work’s title, affected page or file links, your connection to the work and the correction or removal you request. No infringement is intended. We will review rights concerns promptly and remove affected links or material as soon as possible.

Raise a rights concern

Are support form details uploaded to the website?

No. These fields prepare an email draft in your browser. The website does not submit or store their contents. You review and send the message through your own email app. Copying a draft places it on your device’s clipboard.

Still have a question?

Send it our way. Clear questions help us build better answers.
