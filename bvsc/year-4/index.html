4th Year Road Map
  1. Organise Medicine, Surgery and Gynaecology notes by your syllabus topics.
  2. Connect each case discussion with the relevant subject notes and practical record.
  3. Use current clinical guidance and supervision; study notes are not prescribing advice.
Full 4th Year Course Outline (PDF)
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Build problem lists before differential diagnoses, and compare diseases by distinguishing findings rather than isolated facts.
  3. Revisit General and systemic medicine, Infectious and metabolic diseases, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Connect anatomy, patient preparation and procedural reasoning; clinical techniques require supervised training and current guidance.
  3. Revisit Asepsis and surgical principles, Anaesthesiology and pain management, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Compare reproductive timelines by species, then relate abnormal findings to the relevant stage and clinical history.
  3. Revisit Reproductive cycles and breeding, Infertility and reproductive disorders, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Present cases with a concise history, problem list and reasoned plan, and document what needs further confirmation.
  3. Revisit Clinical examination and diagnostic planning, Medical and surgical case discussions, then test your recall without the notes.
