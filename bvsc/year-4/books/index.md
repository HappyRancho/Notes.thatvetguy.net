# Best Books for 4th Year

A focused book list for 4th Year BVSc & AH, organised by subject. Start with one core text, then use a reference for difficult topics.

Choose the edition prescribed by your department. These are reading recommendations; check your college library or a legitimate bookseller for access.

## Veterinary Medicine

### Veterinary Medicine

Peter D. Constable, Kenneth W. Hinchcliff, Stanley H. Done and Walter Grünberg (11th edition)

A substantial large-animal reference for disease mechanisms, diagnosis and management.

### Textbook of Clinical Veterinary Medicine

Amalendu Chakrabarti

A companion for clinical examination and a systematic approach to disease.

[Read Veterinary Medicine notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-4/veterinary-medicine/)

## Veterinary Surgery and Radiology

### Ruminant Surgery

R. P. S. Tyagi and Jit Singh

A species-focused reference to pair with supervised surgical teaching.

### Farm Animal Surgery

Susan L. Fubini and Norm Ducharme (editors)

Use for operative principles and procedure context in farm animals.

[Read Veterinary Surgery and Radiology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-4/veterinary-surgery-and-radiology/)

## Veterinary Gynaecology and Obstetrics

### Veterinary Reproduction and Obstetrics

David E. Noakes, Timothy J. Parkinson and Gary C. W. England (10th edition)

Follow reproductive physiology through fertility, pregnancy and obstetrical problems.

[Read Veterinary Gynaecology and Obstetrics notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-4/veterinary-gynaecology-and-obstetrics/)
