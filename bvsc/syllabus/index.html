Use the outline to plan; use the checklist to track

The checklists reproduce course topics from the Veterinary Council of India’s MSVE 2016 curriculum hosted by DUVASU. They separate theory and practical work and link back to the source. Your university’s current timetable, amendments and examination instructions take precedence.

Ticked topics are saved in this browser on this device. A tick is a personal study marker, not a record of attendance or an official completion certificate.

Why a subject may appear in a different year

The library keeps its existing professional-year navigation. The original first-professional outline includes Biochemistry, while this library places its resources in Year 2. The checklist links to the original source location; use the subject name and course content to resolve the difference.

Turn an outline into a working revision list

Open your subject checklist beside your lecture plan. Identify the topics currently being taught, read the matching notes, then tick a topic when you can explain it without the page in front of you. Keep practical identification, procedures and records separate from theory revision: reading a procedure is not the same as performing it under supervision.
