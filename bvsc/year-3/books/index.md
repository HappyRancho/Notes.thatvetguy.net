# Best Books for 3rd Year

A focused book list for 3rd Year BVSc & AH, organised by subject. Start with one core text, then use a reference for difficult topics.

Choose the edition prescribed by your department. These are reading recommendations; check your college library or a legitimate bookseller for access.

## Veterinary Pharmacology and Toxicology

### Essentials of Veterinary Pharmacology and Therapeutics

H. S. Sandhu

Organise drug classes by mechanism, action and therapeutic use.

[Read Veterinary Pharmacology and Toxicology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-3/veterinary-pharmacology-and-toxicology/)

## Veterinary Parasitology

### Veterinary Parasitology

M. A. Taylor, R. L. Coop and R. L. Wall (4th edition)

Use life cycles, host relationships and diagnostic features as your organising framework.

[Read Veterinary Parasitology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-3/veterinary-parasitology/)

## Veterinary Public Health and Epidemiology

### Veterinary Epidemiology

Michael Thrusfield and Robert Christley

A reference for disease patterns, study design and interpreting diagnostic tests.

[Read Veterinary Public Health and Epidemiology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-3/veterinary-public-health-and-epidemiology/)

## Livestock Products Technology

### Outlines of Dairy Technology

Sukumar De

Support your study of milk processing, preservation and dairy products.

### Meat and Meat Products Technology

B. D. Sharma

Read alongside abattoir and meat-science teaching for processing and product concepts.

[Read Livestock Products Technology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-3/livestock-products-technology/)

## Veterinary and Animal Husbandry Extension Education

### Textbook of Animal Husbandry and Livestock Extension

P. Mathialagan

Connect extension methods with communication and livestock development.

[Read Veterinary and Animal Husbandry Extension Education notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-3/veterinary-and-animal-husbandry-extension-education/)
