Full 3rd Year Course Outline (PDF)
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Organise drug classes by mechanism, indications and adverse effects; verify clinical details against a current professional reference.
  3. Revisit General pharmacology and pharmacokinetics, Systemic pharmacology, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Sketch life cycles with hosts and infective stages, then connect those stages to diagnosis and prevention.
  3. Revisit Helminthology, Veterinary protozoology, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Separate individual clinical findings from population measures, and state the denominator whenever interpreting disease frequency.
  3. Revisit Zoonoses and One Health, Food safety and hygiene, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Use process flow diagrams to connect preservation methods, product quality and the purpose of each processing step.
  3. Revisit Milk processing and dairy products, Meat science and meat products, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Match the communication method to the audience and explain how you would assess whether the advice was understood.
  3. Revisit Extension principles and teaching methods, Rural sociology and communication, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Practise clear histories and structured records under supervision, keeping observation separate from interpretation.
  3. Revisit History taking and clinical examination, Animal handling and hospital routines, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Keep brief observation notes that connect daily routines with animal welfare, production and the reasons behind management decisions.
  3. Revisit Routine animal care and handling, Feeding and housing observations, then test your recall without the notes.
