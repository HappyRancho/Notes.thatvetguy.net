2nd Year Road Map
  1. Use your syllabus to plan Biochemistry, Microbiology and Pathology together.
  2. Keep Genetics and Nutrition calculations beside the worked examples in your notes.
  3. Review practical observations alongside the related theory.
Full 2nd Year Course Outline (PDF)
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Build pathway maps around inputs, outputs and regulation instead of memorising disconnected reaction names.
  3. Revisit Carbohydrate, lipid and protein metabolism, Enzymes and biological oxidation, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Compare each agent by classification, transmission and laboratory identification while keeping species context clear.
  3. Revisit Bacteriology and mycology, Veterinary virology, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Link the underlying mechanism to gross and microscopic changes, then practise describing lesions precisely.
  3. Revisit Cell injury and inflammation, Circulatory disturbances, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Work through inheritance and selection examples step by step, checking assumptions before interpreting the result.
  3. Revisit Mendelian and molecular genetics, Population and quantitative genetics, then test your recall without the notes.
Roadmap
  1. Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
  2. Compare nutrient requirements with feed characteristics and production stage before attempting a balanced ration.
  3. Revisit Feed evaluation and digestion, Energy and protein nutrition, then test your recall without the notes.
