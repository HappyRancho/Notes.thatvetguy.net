# Best Books for 2nd Year

A focused book list for 2nd Year BVSc & AH, organised by subject. Start with one core text, then use a reference for difficult topics.

Choose the edition prescribed by your department. These are reading recommendations; check your college library or a legitimate bookseller for access.

## Veterinary Biochemistry

### Biochemistry

U. Satyanarayana and U. Chakrapani

Use for core pathways and biomolecules; pair with veterinary examples from class.

[Read Veterinary Biochemistry notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-2/veterinary-biochemistry/)

## Veterinary Microbiology

### Veterinary Microbiology and Microbial Disease

P. J. Quinn et al.

Connect organism characteristics with the infections they cause in animals.

[Read Veterinary Microbiology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-2/veterinary-microbiology/)

## Veterinary Pathology

### A Textbook of Veterinary General Pathology

J. L. Vegad

Start with injury, inflammation and repair before moving to individual diseases.

[Read Veterinary Pathology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-2/veterinary-pathology/)

## Animal Genetics and Breeding

### Text Book on Animal Genetics and Breeding

P. Kanakaraj

A subject-focused companion for inheritance, population genetics and breeding principles.

[Read Animal Genetics and Breeding notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-2/animal-genetics-and-breeding/)

## Animal Nutrition

### Animal Nutrition

P. McDonald et al.

A detailed reference for nutrients, digestion and ration-related concepts.

### Principles of Animal Nutrition and Feed Technology

D. V. Reddy

Link nutritional principles with feeds and feed processing.

[Read Animal Nutrition notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-2/animal-nutrition/)
