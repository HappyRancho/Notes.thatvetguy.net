## Livestock Production Management

25 notes

### Books

-

-

[Topic-wise notes](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-1/notes/#livestock-production-management)

Roadmap

- Make a checklist from your university syllabus.
- Compare management requirements by species, age and production stage, with welfare and daily observation in view.
- Revisit Housing and animal handling, Cattle and buffalo management, then test your recall without the notes.

1st Year Road Map 

- Keep your university syllabus beside your subject notes.
- Connect Anatomy structures with Physiology functions; revise diagrams together.
- Make a weekly space for Livestock Production Management and practical records.
