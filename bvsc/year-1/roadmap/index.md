## Your subject playbook

Know what to learn. Know how to revise.

1

### Veterinary Anatomy

Focus on Bones and joints; regional anatomy; histology and embryology.

Try this Learn a structure with its location, relations and function. Redraw it, then identify it on a specimen or slide.

A beautiful diagram with no labels is still an expensive doodle.

2

### Veterinary Physiology

Focus on Blood, cardiovascular and respiratory function; digestion; renal, endocrine and reproductive physiology.

Try this Explain each process as a sequence with feedback. Connect the practical observation to the mechanism it demonstrates.

If you cannot explain the feedback loop, rereading the arrow will not help.

### Livestock Production Management

Focus on Species-wise husbandry, housing, feeding management, handling and farm records.

Try this Compare species and life stages in a small table. Link each management practice to what you observe on the farm.

Do not leave LPM for the evening before the practical.

## A week that works

Suggested rhythm. Adjust it to your timetable.

- 

### After class

Close the notes and explain one process or redraw one labelled structure. Check what you missed.

- 

### After practicals

Finish observations while you remember the specimen, technique or farm visit.

- 

### At the weekend

Revisit older diagrams and one LPM comparison before planning the next week.

## Small habits, big saves

- Keep one main set of notes per subject; add corrections there instead of starting another notebook.
- Look at past professional papers for format and topic coverage, not a promise of repeated questions.
- Keep attendance and practical records up to date; they are not last-night tasks.

Study advice, not an exam rule. A first-person account by Dr. Bhupender (AIEEA Animal Science 2022 Rank 1) informed the concise-revision and error-review habits; his PG strategy is not a professional-exam blueprint.
