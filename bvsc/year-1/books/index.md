# Best Books for 1st Year

A focused book list for 1st Year BVSc & AH, organised by subject. Start with one core text, then use a reference for difficult topics.

Choose the edition prescribed by your department. These are reading recommendations; check your college library or a legitimate bookseller for access.

## Veterinary Anatomy

### Primary Veterinary Anatomy

R. K. Ghosh

A practical starting text for anatomical terminology, organ systems and exam preparation.

### Textbook of Veterinary Anatomy

K. M. Dyce, W. O. Sack and C. J. G. Wensing

Use alongside dissection to compare structures across domestic species.

[Read Veterinary Anatomy notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-1/veterinary-anatomy/)

## Veterinary Physiology

### Dukes’ Physiology of Domestic Animals

William O. Reece et al. (editors, 13th edition)

Build a veterinary foundation in how body systems work and interact.

### Cunningham’s Textbook of Veterinary Physiology

Bradley G. Klein (editor)

A companion for understanding mechanisms before memorising values.

[Read Veterinary Physiology notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-1/veterinary-physiology/)

## Livestock Production Management

### Livestock Production Management

N. S. R. Sastry and C. K. Thomas

Connect housing, handling and routine management with species-specific husbandry.

### A Textbook of Animal Husbandry

G. C. Banerjee

A broader reference for breeds, production systems and farm management.

[Read Livestock Production Management notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-1/livestock-production-management/)
