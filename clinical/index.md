# Clinical Resources

Veterinary handbooks and pharmacology notes for students preparing for clinical work. Start with a reference or explore Medicine, Surgery, Pathology and Pharmacology.

For study, not prescribing. Verify the species, dose and current clinical guidance before treating a patient.

↳ Check the dose. Then check it again. The patient can't.
