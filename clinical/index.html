Questions, answered

Is this a complete veterinary drug index?

No. Drug & Analgesic References currently links to Merck material on NSAIDs and analgesics, rather than individual locally maintained drug monographs.

Where can I study veterinary clinical pathology?

Other Clinical Resources includes Cornell eClinPath, an online clinical pathology textbook covering test interpretation, haematology and chemistry.
