# Vet Apps for Students

Four free apps. Less searching, more studying.

## Free veterinary apps

Study

### Vet Study 2.0

Vet Study

[Get on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.asr.vetstudy2)

A veterinary e-learning companion for keeping revision material close when you are away from your desk.

BVSc revisionQuick reading

Before you installAccess, best fit and a companion resource.Read answerClose answer+−

- Best fit: A quick revision companion between classes. Pair any summary with the unit in your university outline.

- Access: Free to install · contains ads. Content availability may change.

- Keep in mind: Your syllabus remains the checklist for what you must cover. App content can vary by topic and update.[Find your year’s notes ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/)

Reference

### VetConnect

Dr. Ghanasyam Joshi

[Get on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vetconnect.user.vetconnect)

A veterinary drug index for looking up drug and brand information in an Indian practice context.

Drug-name lookupPharmacology revision

Before you installAccess, best fit and a companion resource.Read answerClose answer+−

- Best fit: Connect a brand name with the generic medicine and its pharmacological class when revising.

- Access: Free to install · contains ads. The listing describes offline and online use.

- Keep in mind: A name match is not a treatment decision. Check species, concentration, route and the current product label.[Browse drug references ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/clinical/drug-index/)

Reference

### MSD Vet Manual

Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC

[Get on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.msd.veterinary)

A broad veterinary reference for reviewing diseases, diagnostic approaches and clinical background across species.

Clinical conceptsSpecies comparisons

Before you installAccess, best fit and a companion resource.Read answerClose answer+−

- Best fit: Read the background to a clinical problem: causes, examination findings and how diagnoses are distinguished.

- Access: Free reference. Download the core content first; some media and features need an internet connection.

- Keep in mind: Separate a broad overview from the patient-specific decision. Check species and article references with your supervisor.[Explore clinical handbooks ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/clinical/handbooks/)

Field practice

### IVRI Veterinary Clinical Care

ICAR-IVRI & ICAR-IASRI

[Get on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.icar.ivri.iasri.veterinaryclinicalcareapp)

An Indian field-practice learning aid covering common medicine, gynaecology and surgery presentations.

Livestock casesClinical preparation

Before you installAccess, best fit and a companion resource.Read answerClose answer+−

- Best fit: Prepare for livestock case discussions and connect a presentation with the history and examination you need.

- Access: Free to install. Developed by ICAR-IVRI and IASRI; includes clinical explanations and educational procedure videos.

- Keep in mind: Educational procedure material does not replace supervised skills training or local clinical protocols.[Open final-year resources ](https://notes.thatvetguy.net/bvsc/year-4/)
