## Free apps worth keeping

One job per app. Less scrolling, more learning.

Study

### Vet Study 2.0

Vet Study

A veterinary e-learning companion for keeping revision material close when you are away from your desk.

BVSc revisionQuick reading

A useful way to use it 

Pick one syllabus topic before opening the app. After reading, close it and explain the key points aloud; check gaps against your course outline.

Free to install · contains ads. Content availability may change.

[Get on Google Play ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.asr.vetstudy2)

Reference

### VetConnect

Dr. Ghanasyam Joshi

A veterinary drug index for looking up drug and brand information in an Indian practice context.

Drug-name lookupPharmacology revision

A useful way to use it 

Use it to connect a generic drug to its class and indications. Cross-check species, formulation and current product information with your clinical supervisor.

Free to install · contains ads. The listing describes offline and online use.

[Get on Google Play ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vetconnect.user.vetconnect)

Reference

### MSD Vet Manual

Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC

A broad veterinary reference for reviewing diseases, diagnostic approaches and clinical background across species.

Clinical conceptsSpecies comparisons

A useful way to use it 

Start with a condition from rounds. Read its overview and diagnostic approach, then write a short problem list before discussing the case.

Free reference. Download the core content first; some media and features need an internet connection.

[Get on Google Play ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.msd.veterinary)

Field practice

### IVRI Veterinary Clinical Care

ICAR-IVRI & ICAR-IASRI

An Indian field-practice learning aid covering common medicine, gynaecology and surgery presentations.

Livestock casesClinical preparation

A useful way to use it 

Before a supervised field visit, review one common presentation such as bloat or dystocia. Compare the history, examination and differential diagnoses with what you observe.

Free to install. Developed by ICAR-IVRI and IASRI; includes clinical explanations and educational procedure videos.

[Get on Google Play ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.icar.ivri.iasri.veterinaryclinicalcareapp)

For study and supervised reference. Check species, formulation and current clinical guidance before using drug or treatment information.

## Build a small study setup

Four useful habits beat forty unused apps.

- Start with your syllabus. Open one topic and choose the app that answers that question.
- Download before you travel. Check that the content you need actually opens offline.
- Use active recall. Close the app, explain the concept and check what you missed.
- Keep the source. Record the species, author or publisher and edition where relevant.
