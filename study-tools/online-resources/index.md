## Before you trust a page

A nice PDF cover is not a peer review.

Match your course. Year labels and older TANUVAS course codes can differ from your university’s current outline.

Find the original. Check author, edition, species and references. Use official guidance for regulations and current clinical decisions.

Read beyond the headline. For research, inspect the population, methods and limitations; a search result alone cannot answer a clinical question.

Use AI as a helper. It can organise reading or explain terminology. Check every factual claim and citation before learning it.
