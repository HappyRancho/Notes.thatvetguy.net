## About this book

Primary Veterinary Anatomy introduces gross anatomy for veterinary students. R. K. Ghosh’s text relates the arrangement of organs and body regions to dissection, with comparative material across domestic animals and birds. Use it to build anatomical terminology, identify structures in practical classes and connect systems with their position and relationships.
