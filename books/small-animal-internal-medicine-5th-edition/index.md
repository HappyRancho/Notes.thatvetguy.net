## About this book

A small-animal medicine reference for studying internal diseases in dogs and cats. Use it to connect clinical signs, diagnostic reasoning and disease mechanisms during case-based learning.

For study and supervised reference. Verify the species, dose, edition and current clinical guidance before treating a patient.
